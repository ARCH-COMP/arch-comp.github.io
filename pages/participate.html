Call for Participation

ARCH-COMP — the International Competition on Verifying Continuous and Hybrid Systems — invites tool developers and benchmark proposers across all seven categories. The competition is friendly: within each category, participants jointly agree on benchmarks and metrics, then run their tools on the shared instances.

The Process

1
Deadline:

Join a category group

Pick the category (or categories) that treat problems similar to yours and join that group. Each category is coordinated by a lead who can point you to the current benchmarks and discussion. New participants are always welcome.

2
Deadline:

Agree on benchmarks & submit preliminary results

Each group settles its benchmark set — everybody can propose, and all should approve. Benchmarks already in the ARCH repository are preferred; problems are refined in group discussion. Models and data are collected under goranf/ARCH-COMP/models.

3
Deadline:

Submit your repeatability package

Provide install and run scripts (containerized with Docker is preferred) so your results can be independently reproduced. Submit through the evaluation platform (upload instructions).

4
Deadline:

Prepare the results report

Each category assembles its results report. The evaluation chair confirms that the repeatability packages reproduce the reported results and signals approval.

5

Present & publish

Results are presented at the workshop and published as a category report in the EPiC Series in Computing proceedings (DBLP-indexed). Competition participants are not required to attend the workshop.

Who can take part

Tool Developers

Enter your verification tool in one or more categories and compare against the state of the art on agreed benchmarks.

  • Pick your categories and join the discussion
  • Provide install & run scripts (Docker preferred)
  • Submit a repeatability package

Benchmark Proposers

Contribute challenging, real-world benchmarks that push the tools in a category forward.

  • Coordinate with the category leads
  • Provide models, specifications, and instances
  • Document the expected verification results

New Categories

ARCH-COMP evolves with the community. Proposals for new problem classes are welcome.

  • Sketch the problem class and metrics
  • Line up an initial set of participants
  • Contact the general chairs

Categories & Results

The current categories and their leads are listed on the home page. Competition reports of past years are in the proceedings; repeatability packages of all years are on GitLab.
